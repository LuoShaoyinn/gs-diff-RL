# RX 9070 XT Go2 experiment

Training ran on A843-RX9070xt with Genesis fork `2f5b530582d06241947f640fc2bb2e842ddbd8c4`, Quadrants 1.3.3, and PyTorch `2.11.0+rocm7.2.4.git5fbd98f3` (HIP 7.2.53211). It used the host's existing `srt2` virtual environment and a separate checkout of the pinned Genesis revision; fresh `uv sync` did not complete and was interrupted. The config is 8 environments, 32 steps of 0.01 s (two physics substeps), 100 Adam updates, seed 42, and a fixed batch of 0.2–0.6 m/s commands.

The differentiable training loss fell from 0.273505 at update 0 to 0.107457 at update 99. The mean final forward velocity over the training batch changed from -0.161 to 0.501 m/s; mean final base height was 0.268 m versus its initial 0.42 m. All logged gradient norms were finite. These are training rollouts, not held-out performance; the final checkpoint was saved after the final update, while the final logged metrics describe the rollout before that update.

The longer comparison used the forward-only rigid solver, identical initial poses and a 0.4 m/s command for both environments, with the time input clamped after 32 steps. It is not an exact repeat of the differentiable training dynamics. `evaluation-substeps2.jsonl` reached 0.4 s and then failed with `Invalid constraint forces causing 'nan'`. With four substeps, the trained controller advanced to 0.283 m and its base dropped to 0.195 m at 1.0 s; the same error occurred before the next 0.2 s report. The zero-output controller was at -0.096 m and 0.249 m height at 1.0 s. An eight-substep attempt failed with the same error before the first 0.2 s report. The batch-level error does not identify which environment caused it. These runs do not demonstrate sustained walking or isolate the cause of the numerical instability.

`metrics.jsonl` contains the complete training log. `latest.pt` contains policy and Adam state. Evaluation JSONL files contain periodic successful samples only; the failures are described above.
